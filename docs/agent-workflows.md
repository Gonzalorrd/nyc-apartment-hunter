# Reusable agent workflows

These are documented procedures, not autonomous background agents.

## Discovery
Take target neighborhoods and links. Use permitted public sources/manual browsing.
Find exact building identity and specific units. Record sources and unknowns.
Compare existing identities and listing URLs before insertion. No anti-bot bypass.
Do not turn a historical rent or floorplan starting price into current inventory.
Confirm that the linked offer is a residential apartment rental. Commercial office,
retail and whole-building investment listings cannot establish rental inventory,
even when a mixed-use building also contains apartments. Prefer its residential
StreetEasy page or official leasing site and check bedroom/date availability separately.

## Verification
Open the source; confirm gross rent, fees, concessions, dates, unit ID, bedroom
layout and occupancy. Use the current date checked, not the old listing date.
Preserve source notes and stronger evidence. Changed CSV records need ID/revision.

## Building research
Match exact addresses/towers to public records. Store complaints and reviews as
separate findings with evidence type, date, resolution, severity and confidence.
Do not infer a clean condition from missing records. Flag unresolved contradictions.

## Commute
Use weekday morning departures and the precise destination. Store route components,
source and confidence; missing routing evidence stays unknown. Compare NJ bus, PATH
and ferry options without inventing times. Record a different observation as a new row.

## Evaluation
Apply hard constraints before interpreting scores. Inspect completeness and raw
factors. Explain trade-offs and each occupant's deal breakers. Never select solely
by the largest provisional numeric score. Use evidence-backed manual component ratings.

## Outreach
Find verified leasing channels, draft a concise inquiry, save an editable text draft.
Never send, submit forms, schedule tours or disclose personal information without
explicit authorization. Do not attach or inspect parent-folder documents by default.

## Database maintenance
Run validate, inspect stale records, compare potential aliases, review price/status
changes and preserve audit history. Back up before migrations. Do not delete history
or overwrite manually verified research to make imports easier.
